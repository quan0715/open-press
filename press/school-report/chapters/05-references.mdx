### Reflection

The strongest part of the report is the separation between data and interpretation. The weakest part is the sample itself: because it is invented and small, it is useful only for learning document structure.

If this became a real assignment, the next step would be to replace every sample response with consented evidence, verify the calculations, and rewrite the conclusion around the real results.

#### Submission checklist

- Research question stated clearly
- Method and sample described
- Results separated from discussion
- Limits acknowledged
- References formatted consistently
