## Notes to Demo Data

### 1. Basis of presentation

Northstar Goods Ltd. does not exist. These statements were assembled to demonstrate OpenPress layout, tables, numerical hierarchy, and cross-page consistency.

### 2. Currency and rounding

Amounts are presented in NT$ thousands. Percentages are rounded for display; statement totals use whole-number source values.

### 3. Revenue

Sample revenue is recognized as a single annual value. The example does not model returns, taxes, geographic segments, or timing differences.

### 4. Financial position

Assets equal liabilities plus equity. Cash also agrees with the closing value in the cash-flow summary.

### 5. Use restriction

The report must not be used for investment, lending, valuation, accounting, or tax decisions. It is a fictional design and authoring example.
