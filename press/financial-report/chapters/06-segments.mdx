## Product Mix

The sample revenue story uses three fictional product families.

| Product family | Revenue (NT$000) | Share |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Kitchen tools | 10,400 | 42% |
| Home organization | 8,200 | 33% |
| Repair parts & care | 6,200 | 25% |
| **Total** | **24,800** | **100%** |

### Operating interpretation

Repair parts represent the smallest share but support the fictional promise of longer product life. Kitchen tools remain the largest family, while organization products provide a balanced second source of revenue.

#### Sample priorities for 2026

- Reduce duplicated packaging components.
- Publish clearer repair diagrams.
- Improve demand planning for high-variance parts.
- Keep the product family count stable.
