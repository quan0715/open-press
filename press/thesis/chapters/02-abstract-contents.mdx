## Abstract

This fictional observational study examines how street-tree canopy and building shade could relate to midday pedestrian thermal comfort. A sample dataset of 96 invented observations across eight hypothetical street segments compares shade coverage, surface temperature, and reported comfort. In the demonstration data, segments with more continuous shade show lower surface temperatures and more favorable comfort ratings. The result is illustrative rather than empirical. Its purpose is to demonstrate thesis structure, tables, citations, and cautious interpretation in an editable OpenPress document.

**Keywords:** urban heat, street shade, pedestrian comfort, tree canopy, sample data
